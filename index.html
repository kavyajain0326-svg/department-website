Welcome to the Department of Commerce

Welcome to the official portal of the Department of Commerce at Sri Venkateswara College. Known for academic excellence, our department is dedicated to equipping students with robust analytical, financial, and management skills to excel in the corporate and academic world.

About Department

The Department of Commerce is one of the most vibrant departments in Sri Venkateswara College, fostering a culture of rigorous academic learning combined with holistic student development. We emphasize innovative pedagogy, regular interactive sessions, and industry-aligned training programs.

Courses Offered

The department offers structured undergraduate degrees under University of Delhi guidelines:

  • Bachelor of Commerce (Hons.) [B.Com Hons.]: A comprehensive program focusing on advanced accounting, corporate laws, taxation, and financial systems.
  • Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com Program): Designed to provide broad-based exposure to business, trade, and economic principles.

Academic Activities, Seminars, & Workshops

To bridge the gap between classroom theory and industry exposure, the department regularly hosts multiple events and fests:

  • COMVERSE & CONSORTIUM: Annual flagship departmental fests featuring management games, business quizzes, and speaker sessions.
  • KAAROBAR: The annual commerce festival and publication showcasing student entrepreneurship and articles.
  • Workshops & Add-on Courses: Skill enhancement programs covering tools like AI in finance, stock market training (NISM), and policy-making workshops.
